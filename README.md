### 📌 Featured Commercial Project

#### 📱 [Black Goblin Audio] Onboarding Analytics & Privacy-First Growth Framework
*Privacy-first product analytics & onboarding optimization for an AI-audio deep-tech SaaS (Thol).*

- **Problem:** Open Beta launch faced a stagnant 24-hour Activation Rate (around 10% vs 30% PMF target) with **less existing product telemetry**, leaving product iteration to internal intuition.
- **Data Engineering:** Designed a GDPR-compliant event-driven telemetry pipeline connecting internal servers, PostHog, and BigQuery.
- **Data Integrity & Audit:** Proved credit access was not the bottleneck (68.5 promo mins/mo) and identified a strong negative correlation ($r = -0.71$) between activation status and Time-to-First-Download.
- **Key Discovery:** Identified an over **60% onboarding drop-off** at the initial video upload step. Using **Fisher’s Exact Test** ($p = 3.97 \times 10^{-6}$) and **Odds Ratio Analysis**, proved that uploading a video within 24h increases activation odds by **59.40x**. Triangulated quantitative data with film industry security standards (TPN/MPA) to reveal that users resisted uploading unreleased NDA-protected video files to a 3rd-party cloud.
- **Strategic Impact:** Formulated a 3-phase privacy-first product roadmap (Pre-loaded Sample Assets to slash TTV to <60s, client-side WebAssembly/Wasm, and Local-First Architecture) shifting the company from a "tech-first trap" to product-led growth.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, SQL, PostHog, BigQuery, Supabase, Statistical Hypothesis Testing, Logistic Regression.
